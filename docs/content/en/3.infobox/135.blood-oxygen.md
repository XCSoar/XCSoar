Blood oxygen saturation in percent from a Bluetooth pulse oximeter on Android.
At 90% and above the value uses the normal text colour, from 85% to 89% it is
amber, and below 85% it is red. After 30 seconds the comment line shows the age
of the reading. The value is cleared after three minutes.
