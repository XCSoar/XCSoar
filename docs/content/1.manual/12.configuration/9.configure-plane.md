---
title: Configure Plane
description: Selecting or importing a glide polar and setting the mass, wing area, cruise speed, handicap and ballast of the plane.
---

This dialogue allows the glide polar to be defined. For a large variety of
glider types XCSoar provides a predefined glide polar, they could be modified
if needed, or you can load your own polar from a file.
The file format is based on the WinPilot polar file format (see Section
[Glide polar](/manual/data-files/glide-polar)).

`Config 2/3` → `Plane`

To configure the glide computer to the performance of a glider
type start with the selection of a type from the `List`.
Choose `Import` when you want to load an external polar file.
Customise the three V/W points defining the parabola curve and the reference
weight to your needs.

::tip
Be aware that namely those four items are of crucial importance
for every glide performance relevant computation of XCSoar.
`Export` your efforts to a file: always a good idea.
::

::field-group
  :::field{name="Polar V/W"}
  Three pairs of corresponding horizontal and vertical speed of the glider.
  A good choice for the point triplet is one at the top most area of the
  polar, the second at a still very curved area and the third far out where
  the curvature seems to disappear.
  :::

  :::field{name="Reference mass"}
  Reference weight at which the given polar is valid.
  :::

  :::field{name="Dry mass"}
  The complete weight of the rigged glider including the pilots weight except
  the loaded water ballast.
  In the absence of a configurable pilot weight XCSoar expects you to include
  your own weight to the dry mass.
  :::

  :::field{name="Wing area"}
  Optional specification of the wing area of the glider type.
  :::

  :::field{name="Max. Cruise Speed"}
  Optional upper limit for MacCready speed-to-fly
  (including final glide).  Prevents the glide computer from commanding
  unrealistically high cruise speeds.  A typical choice is the rough-air
  / green-arc limit from the flight manual.
  :::

  :::field{name="Handicap"}
  The handicap factor used for the On-Line Contest scoring.
  :::

  :::field{name="Max. ballast"}
  Optional the amount of water ballast XCSoar refers to as 100% ballast.
  Set to zero if it does not apply.
  :::

  :::field{name="Dump time"}
  The time in seconds needed for dumping full ballast.
  :::
::
