---
title: Aircraft integration
description: "What this chapter covers: XCSoar as a subsystem of the aircraft and its integration with external devices."
---

This chapter discusses XCSoar as a subsystem of the aircraft. It covers the
integration of XCSoar with external devices, including GPS, switches and
sensors, and aircraft radio transceivers and other devices. Integration with
FLARM is covered in Chapter [Airspace, Traffic and Team
Flying](/manual/airspace/capabilities), and integration with variometers is
covered in Chapter [Atmosphere and
Instruments](/manual/atmosphere-and-instruments/atmospheric-model).
