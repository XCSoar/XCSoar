This chapter describes the moving map display as an aid to navigation,
and also describes some of the task and glide related overlays on the
map display.

![Map display elements](/img/figures/fig-map.png)

The moving map shows:

1. Glider, wind indicator, thermal profile, final glide indicator
2. Terrain, relief and hight of the terrain
3. Topography, rivers, roads, towns
4. Waypoints, airports, landables
5. The active task, observation zones, turnpoints
6. The bearing (or route[^1]) to the next waypoint,
   heading
7. Airspaces
8. Markers, thermals history, snail trail
9. Glide range[^2]

The map is drawn in a projected coordinate system (not latitude and
longitude), and the scale can be changed (zooming in and out), as well
as panned.  All navigation functions take the curvature of the Earth
into account.

[^1]: The line to the next waypoint may be a *route*, as described in
    Section [Route](/manual/navigation/route).

[^2]: The glide range is also referred to as the *reach*, as described in
    Section [Glide range line](/manual/navigation/glide-range-line).
