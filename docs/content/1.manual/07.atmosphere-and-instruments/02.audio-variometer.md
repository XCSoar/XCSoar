---
title: Audio Variometer
description: The acoustic variometer on Android devices and where it is enabled.
---

In addition to the variometer display XCSoar provides an acoustic variometer
which transfers the variometer measurements into sounds. The audio variometer
is for now only available on Android devices. For proper performance connect
your device to a barometric sensor or use the internal one of your Android
device if provided.
The audio vario is enabled/disabled via the gauges configuration in the
configuration.
