title: Interfacing with gestures
description: How to draw gestures on the touchscreen to trigger zoom, menus, and dialogues.
---

As of version 6.0, XCSoar supports so-called ‘gestures’.

To use this feature hold down the finger on the touch-screen (or mouse button at
the PC), draw a certain figure and release the touch-screen / mouse button.
Depending on the figure that was drawn a certain function is activated.

![Gesture](/img/figures/gesture1.png)

::gesture-note{id="DR"}
A specific gesture is defined by movements of the finger or the cursor in the
four directions Up, Down, Left and Right. This means if you drag your finger
down and afterwards to the right over the screen, the gesture “DR” is detected,
which stands for “Down-Right”. It will bring up the waypoint list. The manual
indicates an available gesture as shown here on the left side of the text body.
Both the blue hand and pictogram of the movement are used to indicate a specific
gesture, in this case move down then to the right. A list of generally available
gestures is shown below.
::

## Most common or basic gestures:

* :gesture{id="DU" size="sm"} DU, denoting a check mark: Show main menu
* :gesture{id="U" size="sm"} U: Zoom in
* :gesture{id="D" size="sm"} D: Zoom out
* :gesture{id="L" size="sm"} L: Turn screen page pro-grade (Normal, Aux., Full-screen...)
* :gesture{id="R" size="sm"} R: Turn screen page retrograde (Normal, Full-screen...)
* :gesture{id="URDL" size="sm"} URDL, denoting a P: **P**an-mode. Might also be invoked by
  moving two fingers on the screen.  On touchscreens that report both finger
  positions (Android, iOS), pinching zooms the map about the point between the
  fingers, and twisting rotates it temporarily.  A two-finger drag or twist
  enters pan mode, and the temporary rotation is held until pan mode is left; a
  pinch that only zooms keeps following the aircraft.

## More gestures available:

* :gesture{id="DR" size="sm"} DR, denoting an L: Show the Select Waypoint dialogue similar to menu
  item Waypoint **L**ist
* :gesture{id="RD" size="sm"} RD, denoting a T: opens the **T**ask Manager
* :gesture{id="DL" size="sm"} DL: show the Alternates List
* :gesture{id="UD" size="sm"} UD: enables Auto-Zoom
* :gesture{id="ULDR" size="sm"} ULDR: opens the Quick Menu

## Sophisticated dialogues:

* :gesture{id="LDR" size="sm"} LDR, denoting a C: Show the **C**hecklist.
* :gesture{id="URD" size="sm"} URD, denoting an A: Show the **A**nalysis dialogue.
* :gesture{id="LDRDL" size="sm"} LDRDL, denoting an S: Open the **S**tatus dialogue
